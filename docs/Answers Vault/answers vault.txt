HUNTERXJOB CANDIDATE ANSWER POLICY PACK v2.0
PURPOSE
This policy system gives HunterXJob a durable understanding of:
• who the candidate is
• what the candidate is looking for
• what answers are permanently known
• what answers depend on the specific job
• what answers can be derived from verified experience
• what questions require fresh judgment
• what sensitive questions should be skipped
• how to handle differently worded versions of the same question
• how to map an answer into text fields, radio buttons, dropdowns, checkboxes, numbers, dates and multi-selects
• how to avoid inventing information
• how to avoid giving logically contradictory answers
The guiding principle is:
Recognize aggressively. Answer conservatively. Never fabricate.

2. ANSWER MODES
HunterXJob should support more than simple answer or skip.
yaml
answer_modes:

  answer:
    meaning: Use a verified fixed answer.

  derive:
    meaning: Calculate or compose the answer exclusively from verified facts.

  contextual:
    meaning: Answer depends on the job, company, location or application.

  dynamic:
    meaning: Generate a truthful narrative from verified facts and the job description.

  decline:
    meaning: Explicitly answer No / Decline when that is the user's policy.

  skip:
    meaning: Leave optional field unanswered.

  skip_if_optional_else_review:
    meaning: Skip optional field, request review only when mandatory.

  ask_each_time:
    meaning: No permanent answer exists.

  profile_lookup:
    meaning: Retrieve from verified candidate profile.

  application_audit:
    meaning: Determine answer from what actually occurred during this application.

  conditional:
    meaning: Answer only after another question establishes that this field applies.


───

3. FALLBACK TYPES
Do not put every alternative into one fallback array.
There are several fundamentally different fallback classes.
Question aliases
Different ways employers ask the same question.
Example:
yaml
question_aliases:
  - What salary are you looking for?
  - What are your salary expectations?
  - Desired compensation?

Answer fallbacks
Semantically identical answers.
yaml
answer_value: "Yes"

answer_fallbacks:
  - Yes
  - I agree
  - I consent

Format fallbacks
Same information represented differently.
yaml
answer_value: "60000 CAD"

format_fallbacks:
  - "60000"
  - "60,000"
  - "60 000"
  - "$60,000"
  - "60K"

Option fallbacks
Equivalent dropdown choices.
yaml
answer_value: "No"

option_fallbacks:
  - No
  - No, I do not
  - Not applicable

Only use Not applicable when it genuinely means the same thing.
Strategic alternatives
Different legitimate choices approved by the candidate.
yaml
strategic_alternatives:
  - 70000
  - 85000
  - 95000
  - 107000

These are not equivalent answers and should only be selected by salary strategy.
Scope override
A different answer that applies to one company, country or application.
